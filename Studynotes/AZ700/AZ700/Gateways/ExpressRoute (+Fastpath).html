ExpressRoute (+Fastpath)

( ! ) RECONSIDER if:

  • Low- cost/bandwidth 2 VNet  > VPN Gateway (S2S).

  • 3rd party Cloud/SD-WAN without ER-Direct > IPsec VPN.

  • Public PaaS/SaaS (No dedicated circuit) > Public Internet.

EXAM:
Connect On-Prem Site A to On-Prem Site B over ER" ➔ ExpressRoute Global Reach (Premium SKU).
"Access O365 but limit to SharePoint only" ➔ Microsoft Peering + Route Filter (Select SharePoint Community).
"Gateway Subnet UDR not working" ➔ FastPath is enabled (Bypassing Gateway) or ER Direct is missing.
"Need sub-second link failure detection" ➔ Enable BFD on your on-premises edge devices.
//(Bidirectional Forwarding Detection)
"100 VNets connected to one circuit" ➔ ExpressRoute Premium.
