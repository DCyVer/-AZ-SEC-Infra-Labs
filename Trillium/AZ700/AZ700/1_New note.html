2. Load Balancer: The "Move to Standard" Rule
Since Basic is retired, the exam tests why you must use Standard.

  • Trigger: Private Link, Global Peering, or HA Ports 

  • Standard.

  • Trigger: "Inbound Flow" security (NSG required) 

  • Standard.

  • Note: If the scenario has > 100 VMs, it's always Standard.

5. VPN Gateway: The "Performance" Logic
Don't memorize the numbers; memorize the Gen1 vs. Gen2 cut-off.

  • VpnGw1 to 3 = Gen1 (Basic/Old).

  • VpnGw4 to 5 = Gen2 (Required for 5Gbps+ or 64-bit support).

    • "BGP" or "Multiple S2S tunnels" 

  • VpnGw1 or higher (Never Basic).

The AZ-700 "One-Liner":
If the requirement is Global, Secure-by-default, or High-Throughput, the answer is always the highest available SKU (Standard/Premium/V2).
